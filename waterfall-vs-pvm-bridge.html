Waterfall chart vs Price-Volume-Mix bridge

Use a waterfall (bridge) chart to show which items added to or took away from a total – by product, region or channel. Use a price-volume-mix bridge when you need to explain why the total moved: more or fewer units, a shift in mix between segments, or a change in value per unit.

Common questions

Is a PVM bridge a type of waterfall chart?

Yes. A price-volume-mix bridge is a waterfall with three fixed steps – volume, mix and rate – between two totals, instead of one step per item.

What is the mix effect?

The change in value caused by a shift in the share of units between segments that earn different amounts per unit, holding each segment's rate at the earlier level.
