Privacy and cookies

Short version: this site only uses Google Analytics if you say yes, and the chart scripts never collect anything.

Analytics

When you first visit, a banner asks whether you're happy for Google Analytics to run. Until you click Accept analytics, it isn't loaded at all and no cookies are set. If you accept, Google Analytics 4 records things like which pages are viewed, roughly where visitors are (country or city level), the device and browser, and how people arrived (search, a link, direct). It's used only to see which charts and guides are useful. Advertising features and ad personalisation are switched off.

Google Analytics sets cookies named _ga and _ga_… that last up to two years. They're shared with kaijxnes.com, which is the same owner. The data is processed by Google – see how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Changing your choice

Your choice is saved in your browser (not in a cookie). You can change it any time: . Declining after accepting switches analytics off and deletes its cookies from this site.

The chart scripts

The chart scripts you embed in a dashboard don't include analytics, set no cookies and send no data anywhere. They read the grid on the page in the viewer's browser and draw the chart there. The only requests they make are to download the chart script and Chart.js.

Other things the site loads

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, and the site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security. No other tracking is used.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data: get in touch through kaijxnes.com.
