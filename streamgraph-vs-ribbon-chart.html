Chart comparison

Streamgraph vs ribbon chart vs stacked bars

All three show a total split into segments over time. Stacked bars are the most exact and easiest to read. A ribbon chart re-ranks the segments every period, so changes in rank stand out. A streamgraph is the most organic-looking and good for storytelling with many periods, but the hardest to read exact values from.

Common questions

What is the difference between a streamgraph and a stacked area chart?

A streamgraph is a stacked area chart centred on a middle line instead of sitting on zero, which makes the layers flow more evenly but removes a common baseline.

What is a ribbon chart?

A stacked column chart where the segments are sorted by size within every column and joined by ribbons, so you can follow each segment's rank and size over time.
