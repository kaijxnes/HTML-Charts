Box plot vs histogram vs ridgeline

Use a histogram to see the full shape of one distribution. Use a box plot to compare the median and spread of several groups side by side. Use a ridgeline when you want to compare the full shapes of several groups, or of one value across months.

Common questions

Which is better, a box plot or a histogram?

Neither – a histogram shows the shape of one distribution in detail; a box plot summarises many distributions compactly for comparison.

What is a ridgeline plot?

Several distribution curves stacked vertically with a slight overlap, like a mountain range, used to compare the shapes of many groups.
