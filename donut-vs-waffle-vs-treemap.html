Chart comparison

Donut chart vs waffle chart vs treemap

All three show parts of a whole. A donut works for a few segments (up to five or six). A waffle chart makes percentages literally countable – 100 squares – and is easier to read than a donut. A treemap handles many items and hierarchies, where donuts and waffles break down.

Common questions

Are donut charts bad?

Not for a few segments, but people judge angles poorly, so close values are hard to compare. Waffle charts or bars are easier to read exactly.

When should I use a treemap?

When there are many items or a hierarchy (e.g. vertical then product), since rectangles scale to dozens of segments.
