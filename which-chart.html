Common questions

What chart should I use to show change over time?

A line chart for one or two metrics; a combo chart for a total and a rate; a year-over-year chart when there is seasonality; a rolling 12-month chart to see the underlying trend.

What chart should I use to show a split over time?

Stacked bars for exact values, a mix shift chart for changing shares, a ribbon chart for rank changes and a streamgraph for a flowing overview.

What chart shows why revenue changed?

A waterfall (bridge) chart by item, or a price-volume-mix bridge to split the change into volume, mix and price/rate.
