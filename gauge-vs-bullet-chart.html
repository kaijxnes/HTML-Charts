Gauge vs bullet chart vs goal rings

Gauges are familiar and work for one or two headline numbers. Bullet charts show the same thing – actual against target with performance bands – in a fraction of the space, so they are better for many items. Goal rings are a compact, eye-catching way to show a handful of targets together.

Common questions

Why use a bullet chart instead of a gauge?

A bullet chart shows actual, target and performance bands in a single thin bar, so many can be stacked and compared – gauges take a lot of space for one number.

What are goal rings?

Concentric progress rings where a full circle is 100% of target, similar to fitness activity rings.
